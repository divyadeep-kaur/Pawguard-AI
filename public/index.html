      Know what to do

      Quick first-aid & safety guide

      🩸 Bleeding

      Press a clean cloth firmly on the wound. Do not remove it if soaked; add more on top. Keep the animal calm and still.

      🦴 Suspected fracture

      Do not straighten or lift the limb. Move the animal only if it is in traffic, using a blanket as a stretcher.

      🌡️ Heatstroke

      Move to shade, offer small sips of water, wet the paws and belly with cool (not icy) water.

      ⚠️ Stay safe

      Injured animals can bite from fear. Keep distance, avoid the head, wear gloves if you have them, never force food.

      Emergency contacts (India)

      • 1962National animal ambulance helpline (available in many states)
      • 112General emergency number
      • AWBIAnimal Welfare Board of India, for cruelty complaints
